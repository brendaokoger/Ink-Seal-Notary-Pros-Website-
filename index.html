    Frequently Asked Questions

    Common questions.

    An apostille is an official government certification that authenticates a document for use in foreign countries that are members of the Hague Apostille Convention. It verifies the authenticity of the signature, seal, or stamp — not the document's content — so it will be legally recognized abroad.

    If your destination country is a Hague Convention member, you need an apostille. If the country is not a member, you need embassy or consulate legalization — a multi-step process. We confirm which process applies to your situation before you submit.

    Yes. We review your submission first and provide a clear, itemized quote before any processing begins. You approve the quote before we proceed. No hidden fees, no surprises.

    Standard processing through the Illinois Secretary of State typically takes 10–15 business days. Expedited options (3–5 business days) are available for time-sensitive documents at an additional fee. We confirm current turnaround times at submission and can advise on the fastest option for your situation.

    Common documents include birth certificates, death certificates, marriage and divorce certificates, FBI background checks, court orders, corporate documents, powers of attorney, school transcripts, and notarized documents. If you are unsure whether your document qualifies, submit it through our intake form and we will confirm eligibility before any processing begins.

    Yes. We offer Remote Online Notarization (RON) via secure two-way video — no in-person visit required. Our online notary services are available to clients in Illinois and Indiana and are widely accepted across the United States.

    No appointment is needed to get started with apostille, online notarization, or translation services. Submit your information through our intake form and we will reach out within one business day with next steps and a quote. In-person FD-258 fingerprinting sessions require a scheduled appointment.

    Yes. For apostille services, you mail your original documents to us. We submit them to the Secretary of State and return everything via trackable mail once processing is complete. Online notarization clients complete the entire process digitally — no mailing required.
