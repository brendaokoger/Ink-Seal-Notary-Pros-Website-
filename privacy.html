How we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit this website, submit a request, or contact us.

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

Ink & Seal Notary Pros (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website and through phone or text contact, how we use it, when we may share it, and the choices available to you. It describes our current practices only.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, primarily through the forms on this website and through phone calls or text messages to (312) 967-7696:

  • Contact information — your name, email address, phone number, and, where requested, your state of residence.
  • Contact form submissions — when you use the general inquiry form on our Contact page, we collect your name, email, phone number, preferred contact method, the service you’re asking about, and the message you write.
  • Service intake information — when you request Apostille coordination, Remote Online Notarization, FD-258 Fingerprinting, or Certified Translation, we collect what’s needed to process that specific request, such as document type, destination or issuing state, scheduling preferences, and, for translation requests, source and target language and page count.
  • Electronic signature data — your typed name and date, submitted as an electronic signature on certain forms.
  • Calls and text messages — your phone number and the content of a call or text you send to (312) 967-7696. See “Phone Calls & Text Messages” below.
  • Uploaded documents — files you upload through an intake form. See “Document Uploads” below.
  • Usage data — general, non-identifying information about how you interact with our website, such as browser type and pages visited, collected automatically by our hosting provider.

Phone Calls & Text Messages

You may reach Ink & Seal Notary Pros by calling or texting (312) 967-7696. When you do, we receive your phone number and the content of your call or message so we can respond to you and provide the assistance you asked for.

Calling or texting us is a customer-service communication. It does not automatically enroll you in a recurring marketing or promotional text messaging program, and we do not send marketing text messages simply because you contacted us. If that were to change, we would provide appropriate notice and obtain any consent required by law before doing so. Standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

Document Uploads

When you upload documents through one of our intake forms, those files are transmitted to us and stored in a private, access-controlled Google Drive folder for the sole purpose of completing your service request. Uploaded documents are not shared with third parties beyond what’s described in this policy, are not used for any other purpose, and are not retained beyond what’s necessary to complete your service or as required by law.

Please only upload documents that are necessary for your request, and avoid including sensitive financial information — such as full bank account numbers, credit card numbers, or tax identification numbers — unless it is specifically required for the service you’re requesting.

Payment Information

We do not collect or store payment information through this website, and this site does not use an online payment processor. You will never be asked to enter card or banking information into our online forms. Payment for services is arranged directly between you and Ink & Seal Notary Pros outside of this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to inquiries submitted through our Contact page or by phone or text.
  • Review, quote, and process your Apostille, notarization, fingerprinting, or translation request.
  • Contact you about your request — by phone, text, or email — including appointment details, session invitations, or follow-up.
  • Maintain accurate records of the services we provide.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, or notarial recordkeeping requirements.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising purposes.

When We Share Information

We share information only where necessary to provide the service you requested:

  • Government agencies — for example, submitting your documents to a Secretary of State’s office as part of apostille processing.
  • Service providers who support our operations — described in “Third-Party Services” below, who process or store information on our behalf.
  • Remote Online Notarization sessions — if you complete a Remote Online Notarization, you are connected to a secure third-party video notarization platform to complete identity verification and the notarial act itself, in accordance with applicable notary law. That platform has its own privacy practices, separate from this website.
  • As required by law — if we are legally required to disclose information, such as in response to a valid legal request.

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies & Browser Storage

This website does not set traditional tracking cookies. We use limited browser session storage for basic functionality only — for example, to avoid replaying an introductory animation you’ve already seen, or to briefly remember a selection (such as a document destination) as you move between pages. This information is stored only on your own device, is automatically cleared when you close your browser, and is never transmitted to us or shared with anyone.

Analytics & Tracking

We do not currently use analytics platforms, advertising pixels, or other behavioral tracking technologies on this website.

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of third-party services to operate this website and process the information described above:

  • Vercel — hosts this website.
  • Google Drive — securely stores documents uploaded through our intake forms.
  • Google Sheets / Google Workspace — securely receives and stores information submitted through our request forms so our team can review and follow up.
  • Formspree — processes submissions for some of our forms.
  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used on this website; your browser may contact Google’s servers directly to load these fonts.

These providers process information on our behalf and maintain their own privacy and security practices. We encourage you to review those practices directly if you have questions beyond what’s described here.

Information Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information transmitted through this website and stored in connection with your service request, and we limit access to information you submit to those who need it to complete your request. That said, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain the information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to complete your request, respond to your inquiry, and satisfy any legal, tax, or notarial recordkeeping obligations that apply to our services. Uploaded documents are handled as described in “Document Uploads” above. We do not retain information longer than necessary for these purposes.

Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. We will respond to valid requests within a reasonable timeframe. Depending on where you live, applicable state law may provide you with additional privacy rights; we will honor valid requests to the extent required by law.

Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

External Links

Our website may link to third-party websites — for example, a government agency’s site or one of the service providers described above. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies directly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. When we do, we will revise the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
