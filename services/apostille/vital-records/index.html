Apostille Services ◆ Vital Records Apostille

Birth, Marriage & Death Certificate Apostille

◆

Vital records — birth certificates, marriage certificates, and death certificates — are among the most commonly requested documents for apostille certification. Whether you're pursuing dual citizenship, getting married abroad, handling an international estate, or establishing residency in a foreign country, an apostille on your vital records is often a required step.

Who Needs a Vital Records Apostille

  • Individuals applying for dual citizenship or foreign residency
  • Couples marrying in a foreign country who need US vital records authenticated
  • Families managing international estate or inheritance matters
  • Individuals adopting internationally or navigating immigration processes
  • Students submitting foreign university applications that require authenticated personal records

Common Vital Records for Apostille

  • US birth certificates (federal or state-issued)
  • Marriage certificates
  • Death certificates
  • Divorce decrees and dissolution orders
  • Adoption records

What You'll Need

  • A certified copy of your vital record (not a photocopy) — issued directly by the government authority that holds the original record
  • The name of the destination country where the apostilled document will be used
  • Any special formatting or translation requirements from the receiving institution

The Vital Records Apostille Process

1
Document Submission

Provide a certified copy of your vital record along with the destination country and any instructions from the receiving institution.

2
Eligibility Review

We confirm the document is eligible for apostille and identify the correct issuing state authority — since vital records must be apostilled by the state where they were issued.

3
Notarization (if needed)

Some vital records may require additional notarization depending on the document type and destination country requirements.

4
State Processing

The document is submitted to the appropriate Secretary of State for apostille authentication.

5
Return

Your authenticated document is returned to you via tracked shipping with confirmation of completed processing.

A Note on Certified Copies

Apostilles are issued on certified copies of vital records — not photocopies. If you don't have a certified copy, we can help guide you through requesting one from the appropriate issuing authority before your apostille can be processed.
