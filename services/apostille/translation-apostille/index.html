Certified Document Translation + Apostille

◆

Some international document requirements call for both apostille authentication and certified translation — either because a US document must be translated into a foreign language before use abroad, or because a foreign-language document submitted in the US needs both translation and authentication. Ink & Seal Notary Pros coordinates both services through a single, streamlined process.

Who Needs Both Translation and Apostille

  • Individuals submitting US documents to foreign institutions that require a translated and apostilled copy
  • Clients with foreign-language vital records (birth, marriage, or death certificates) needing certified translation before use in the US
  • International students and immigrants with foreign diplomas or transcripts that require certified translation
  • Legal professionals managing matters that cross both linguistic and jurisdictional boundaries
  • Businesses with international partners that require authenticated and translated agreements or corporate records

Common Scenarios

  • US birth certificate apostilled and translated into Spanish, French, or another language for use in a foreign country
  • Foreign marriage certificate translated into English and apostilled for use in the US
  • US corporate documents apostilled and translated for international business transactions
  • Foreign academic records or diplomas requiring certified translation alongside an apostilled supporting document
  • Notarized powers of attorney requiring both translation and apostille for use abroad

The Coordinated Process

1

Document Assessment

We review your documents and confirm exactly what combination of services is needed — translation, notarization, apostille, or all three.

2

Certified Translation

Professional certified translation is arranged for any documents requiring it. Translations include a signed certification of accuracy and completeness, required for legal, governmental, and institutional use.

3

Notarization

Translation certifications and any supporting documents are notarized as required by the destination country's or institution's specifications.

4

Apostille Processing

The notarized translation and original documents are submitted to the appropriate state or federal authority for apostille authentication.

5

Coordinated Return

All translated and apostilled documents are returned together via tracked shipping — one coordinated package, not a piecemeal process.

About Certified Translations

Certified translations provided through this service are prepared by qualified translators and include a signed certification of accuracy and completeness. This certification is required for most legal, governmental, and institutional purposes — including USCIS immigration filings, court submissions, foreign consular documents, and academic records.

A certified translation is not the same as a machine translation or a notarized translation of your own work. It is a professional translation with a formal attestation, and for many purposes it must accompany an apostilled document to be accepted.
