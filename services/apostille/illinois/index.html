Illinois Apostille Services

◆

Illinois documents headed abroad — whether for immigration, international business, education, or personal matters — often require apostille certification before a foreign government or institution will accept them. As a state-commissioned Illinois notary, Ink & Seal Notary Pros handles each Illinois apostille request with precision and discretion.

Who This Service Is For

  • Illinois residents with documents issued in Illinois
  • Non-residents with Illinois-issued vital records or corporate documents
  • Attorneys managing client matters that involve Illinois documentation
  • Businesses with Illinois entity documents required for international transactions

Common Illinois Documents

  • Illinois birth certificates (from Illinois Department of Public Health)
  • Illinois marriage and death certificates
  • Illinois notarized documents (contracts, affidavits, powers of attorney)
  • Illinois Secretary of State corporate filings and certificates of good standing
  • Illinois court documents and legal records
  • Illinois educational transcripts and diplomas (when issued by an Illinois institution)

The Illinois Apostille Process

1
Document Review

We evaluate your document and confirm apostille eligibility, identifying any additional steps required before processing can begin.

2
Notarization (if required)

Some Illinois documents require notarization before the Secretary of State will issue an apostille. We coordinate this step as part of the process when needed.

3
Illinois Secretary of State Processing

Your document is submitted to the Illinois Secretary of State for apostille authentication — the official state-level certification that validates the document for international use.

4
Return & Delivery

Your authenticated document is returned to you via tracked shipping, with handling notes and confirmation of completed processing.
