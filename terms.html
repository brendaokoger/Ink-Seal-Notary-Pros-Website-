Failure to meet identity verification or other session requirements may result in the session being declined or cancelled. RON sessions are subject to eligibility requirements, including the signer’s location at the time of the session and the requirements of the state under which the notarization is performed.

We do not guarantee that a notarized document will be accepted by the recipient, institution, or authority you submit it to. It is your responsibility to confirm any specific notarization or formatting requirements with the receiving party in advance.

In-person FD-258 ink fingerprinting sessions require a scheduled appointment. We provide professionally rolled fingerprint impressions on the FD-258 card format. We do not guarantee that your fingerprint card will be accepted, legible to, or processed by the requesting agency, employer, or licensing body — quality and acceptance can also depend on individual factors such as skin condition, and requirements set by the receiving agency are outside our control.
